Frequently asked questions

Is CV Maker Pro+ free? How does pricing work?

Downloading the app is free, and you can start building your CV right away. Premium features such as PDF export and the AI tools are offered through a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription. Current prices for your country are shown on the purchase screen in the App Store. Subscriptions are managed through your App Store account and can be cancelled at any time.

Where is my data stored? Is my privacy protected?

Your CV data is stored locally on your device first — no account is required. When you use the AI features, only the relevant text is processed securely to generate your response, and it is not used to train models. The usage statistics we collect to improve the app are anonymous and can be switched off entirely in Settings. Read our privacy policy for details.

Can I import my existing CV (PDF)?

Yes. When you upload your current CV as a PDF, the app reads the text and automatically places it into sections such as experience, education and skills. You can then strengthen the content with AI and export it with a fresh template.

What does ATS compatibility mean? How does the app help?

Many companies first scan applications with software called an ATS (applicant tracking system). If that software cannot parse your CV, your application may never reach human eyes. CV Maker Pro+ produces PDFs that ATS software can read reliably, and improves your content with keyword and section suggestions.

Which languages are supported?

The app is available in 6 languages: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Portuguese. You can build your CV in any of them, and the AI tools work across all supported languages.
