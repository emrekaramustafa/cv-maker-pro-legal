Questions, answered honestly

Will a high ATS score get me through every applicant tracking system?

No, and no tool can promise that. Every company configures its ATS differently, and many decisions are made by people. Our score checks what can be verified: that a parser can find your contact details, titles, dates, bullets and skills, and that the layout is single-column. That removes avoidable failures; it does not guarantee an interview.

How is the match % calculated?

It is deterministic, not a guess from an AI. We compare the posting’s job title with your target and recent titles (50%), check which of your skills appear in the posting (30%) and how many of the posting’s most frequent terms your CV mentions (20%). A posting for a different role is capped at 45%. The app and the website use exactly the same formula.

Where do the job postings come from?

From public job boards and their feeds, such as Himalayas, Jobicy, Arbeitnow and Remote OK; every card names its source. We show a short snippet and send you to the original posting to apply. We do not edit postings and cannot vouch for every employer, so check a posting the way you normally would.

What happens to my CV?

On the website your CV is saved only in your browser’s local storage. A job search sends just your target title and location. Tailoring sends your CV text and the posting to our AI service for that one request. “Continue in the app” stores your CV on our server for up to 24 hours, and it is deleted as soon as the app reads it.

Is it free?

The ATS report and job matches are free on the web, and you can tailor your CV to 2 postings a day. PDF export, job alerts and unlimited tailoring live in the app; some of them are part of its Premium subscription.

How do I move my CV into the app?

In the web app, open “Get the app” and create a code. In the app, choose “Import from web” and type the 6-character code. The code works once and expires after 24 hours.
